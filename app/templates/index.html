Model Card & Operational Scope
PurifEye v0.1 · YOLOv8 Nano · CPU-only · river plastic detection
Mission & Architecture

Mission: Automated detection and quantification of floating plastic bottles in freshwater riverways to prevent marine plastic discharge at the source.

Hardware Profile: Designed strictly for standard CPU and edge hardware. Zero GPU dependencies. Multi-threaded consumer-grade architectures using Python threading and queue.Queue.

Inference Stack: YOLOv8 Nano (Ultralytics 8.4) + PyTorch CPU backend. Edge: ONNX Runtime, OpenVINO, ai-edge-litert TFLite.

Known Limitations & Boundaries
  • ◈ Extreme specular water glare under direct vertical midday sun reduces detection confidence.
  • ◈ Heavy industrial foam rafts or biological mats can obscure bottle contours and cause false negatives.
  • ◈ Low-light nocturnal surveillance is out-of-scope (dataset collected exclusively in daylight conditions).
  • ◈ Submerged or heavily algae-coated clear PET bottles with matching water refractive index are likely missed.
